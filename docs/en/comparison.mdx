There are plenty of ways to look at your LAN. Most fall into three camps: **cloud apps**
(Fing), **desktop scanners** (Advanced IP Scanner, Angry IP Scanner), the **CLI** (Nmap), and
**self-hosted monitors** (NetAlertX / Pi.Alert). MyNeS sits in the last camp — but adds the
things those tools leave out: non-IP discovery, AI identification, a security/CVE view, first-class
Home Assistant support, and a native mobile client.

## What makes MyNeS different

1. **It sees the devices an IP scan can't.** ARP and ping only find things with an IP. MyNeS also listens to mDNS/Bonjour, SSDP/UPnP, **Bluetooth LE** (no IP at all) and **Zigbee/Z-Wave over MQTT** — so smart-home gear that other scanners miss actually shows up. See [Features → Discovery](/features#discovery).
2. **It explains what it found.** Vendor lookup, OS and connection-medium guessing, active fingerprinting, and optional **AI identification** turn a wall of IPs into named, typed devices. See [Configuration → AI](/configuration#ai-assisted-identification-optional).
3. **It watches and warns.** Scheduled scans diff over time and alert on new devices, MAC/IP changes, newly opened ports and health signals — across Web Push, Home Assistant, ntfy, Telegram, Slack, Discord, webhook and email. Plus a fleet **security & CVE** view. See [Features → Monitoring](/features#monitoring--alerts).
4. **It fits a home lab.** Multi-arch Docker (runs on a Raspberry Pi), a one-click **Home Assistant add-on**, Prometheus / InfluxDB / Grafana, a full REST API, and a native **mobile** client — all self-hosted, no cloud, no telemetry.

## When another tool is the better fit

Honest guidance beats a rigged table:

- **You want a one-off port/OS deep-scan from a terminal** → Nmap is the gold standard (MyNeS uses it under the hood).
- **You just want a quick device list on Windows** → Advanced IP Scanner is fast and zero-setup.
- **You want a tiny cross-platform desktop scanner** → Angry IP Scanner is lightweight and open source.
- **You want a polished phone app with a cloud account and don't mind closed source** → Fing is well made.
- **You want continuous home-network monitoring, non-IP discovery, security insight and Home Assistant** → that's exactly what MyNeS is for.
